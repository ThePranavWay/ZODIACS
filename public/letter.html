An index card, yellow, adhesive strip intact. Eighteen words in typewriter face. No signature, no date, no return address. The words are not any language the cryptographers on file recognise — but the numbers in the note are not part of the chaff.

reading rule The words are cover text. Only the numeric groups were ever meant to be read — take them in the order the eye travels across the card.
